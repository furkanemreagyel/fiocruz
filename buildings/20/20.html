The Aquarium:

The Aquarium, constructed in 1909 by architect Luiz Moraes Jr., was a specialized facility within the Manguinhos campus dedicated to the study of aquatic flora and fauna. Designed to support research into waterborne diseases and biological diversity, it formed a key part of the institution’s early scientific infrastructure. In the 1960s, during a period of campus reorganization, the building, despite its unique contribution to the eclectic architectural ensemble of the site, faced demolition. Archival records today remember it as a symbol of the Institute's historical commitment to comprehensive biological exploration.
