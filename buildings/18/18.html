Carlos Chagas Pavilion

The building was designed in 1944 by architect Olenka Freire Greve, from the Works Division of the Ministry of Education and Health, and involved the active participation of the director of the Oswaldo Cruz Institute at the time, Dr. Henrique Aragão, who liked to follow the progress of the constructions on the Manguinhos campus. Construction began in 1946, and it was inaugurated in 1951. It features functional architecture without aesthetic flourishes, following the modern typology of vertical monoblock hospitals. It is also known as the Pathology Pavilion.
