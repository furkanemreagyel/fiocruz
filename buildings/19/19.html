Lauro Travassos Pavilion

The building was constructed in the 1930s after a period without construction in Manguinhos and is one of the first built under the Ministry of Education and Public Health, therefore not being designed by the architect Luiz Moraes Jr. Its original facilities, begun in 1937 with only one floor, underwent numerous additions over the years to serve as an animal facility. After the construction of the Central Animal Facility (current Cecal), this pavilion came to be known as the Old Animal Facility or Former Animal Facility, until it was given its current name, the Lauro Travassos Pavilion.
