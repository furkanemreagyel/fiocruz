Manguinhos Archaeological Site

Inscribed by Iphan in the National Register of Historical Sites in 1966 based on archaeological research carried out according to a revealed prehistoric archaeological potential on the occasion of the collapse of an elevated barrier near Av. Brasil due to heavy rain. More recent research has also identified archaeological remains of an old stable and the old urban waste incineration oven, incorporated into the visitation circuit of Fiocruz's Museum of Life.

Reference: Instituto do Patrimônio Histórico e Artístico Nacional. (1966). Escavações no Sítio Arkeológico de Manguinhos [Field photograph]. IPHAN Archive.
