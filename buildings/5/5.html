Casa de Chá

Completing the eclectic ensemble of Pasteur Square are the Tea House (c.1905) and the Annex Restaurant (c.1920). Built to be the cafeteria for the employees of the Oswaldo Cruz Institute, the Tea House was originally configured as an arbor with hollow hardwood panels and a roof interrupted by the presence of trees inside, whose crowns provided shade for the building. The arbor, along with the annex built later next to it in masonry and with a ceramic tile roof, functioned as a place for meals and a meeting point for scientists until the early 1950s, when the Central Restaurant was inaugurated on campus. Over the course of the year, the Tea House has undergone several changes. The main one being the removal of the internal trees and the modification of the roof. The Tea House and Annex still function today as a restaurant.
