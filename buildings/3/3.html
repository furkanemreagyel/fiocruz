Cavalariça

The Stables (1904), also influenced by English architecture, was designed to house horses used in the manufacture of serums. It is interesting to note that the Cavalariça’s infrastructure was designed to make full use of the waste generated by the animals, a fact that demonstrates the institution’s interest not only in technological innovations but also in rationally sustainable solutions. In the external volume, elements such as the skylight stand out, which allows the entry of natural light into the interior of the building, which is currently one of the exhibition areas of the Fiocruz Life Museum.

Reference: Oliveira, B. R. (2018). A Cavalariça e a produção de soros em Manguinhos [Photograph]. Fiocruz Imagens. https://portal.fiocruz.br/en/node/1402
