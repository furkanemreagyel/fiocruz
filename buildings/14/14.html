Rocha Lima Pavilion

The building was designed by architect Waldyr Ramos, from the Works Division of the Ministry of Health, and built between 1956 and 1965. The building followed the vertical monoblock typology adopted by the Works Division during that period for the construction of health institutions. Currently, it houses the headquarters of Bio-Manguinhos.

The unit has a strategic role for the Ministry of Health. Provide to the population essential products for the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of diseases. It is one of the largest public producers of vaccines in Latin America, which reach the health centers through the National Immunization Program. Its products help protect against yellow fever, polio, meningitis A and C, measles, mumps, and rubella (MMR), rotavirus, Haemophilus influenzae type b (Hib)—a bacteria that can cause various diseases, including meningitis, which is very serious—and against pneumococcus bacteria
