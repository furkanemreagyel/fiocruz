Evandro Chagas Hospital

The building was designed between 1912 and 1918 by the architect Luiz Moraes Jr. The original project included six pavilions, but only one was built. In the 1950s, another building was designed by Ramiro Pereira, from the Works Division of the Ministry of Health, to replace the original facilities of the old hospital and was named Hospital Evandro Chagas, which remained so from 1956 to 1980. Since 1989, it has been protected by federal law (IPHAN) along with the other buildings comprising the Manguinhos Historical Architectural Nucleus (NAHM). Other names related to the 1956 pavilion are Pavilion 26. Between 1959 and 1978, the Oswaldo Cruz Hospital was known as the Gaspar Vianna Pavilion.
