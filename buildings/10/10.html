The Brasil Avenue Entrance building

Authored by Nabor Foster, it was built to consolidate new access to the campus and organize the flow of people and vehicles as a result of the opening of Brazil Avenue in the 1940s. The project adheres to the contemporary architectural style of the campus pavilions constructed since the 1940s. Inepac is currently analyzing its listing, much like the Henrique Arago Pavilion.

Reference: Foster, N. (1955). Portaria da Avenida Brasil: Arquitetura moderna em Manguinhos [Archival image]. Casa de Oswaldo Cruz.
