Leonidas Deane Pavilion

The Leônidas Deane Pavilion (Building 26) was originally designed in 1956 to serve as a replacement for the old Oswaldo Cruz Hospital, though it remained under construction for decades. Initially known as the Evandro Chagas Hospital between 1956 and 1980, it was finally inaugurated in the 2000s and renamed in honor of the renowned parasitologist Leônidas de Oliveira Deane. Today, the pavilion serves as a critical hub for high-level research, housing units such as the Clinical Immunology Laboratory and providing vital infrastructure for teaching and scientific activities within the Oswaldo Cruz Institute.
