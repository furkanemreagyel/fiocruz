Quinino

The Figueiredo de Vasconcelos Pavilion (1919), or Quinino, was built to house the Official Medicines Service and initially housed the laboratories for the production of quinine (used to prevent malaria). The original project was just two floors, with proportions very similar to those of Stable, located on the opposite side of  Pasteur Square. During the 1940s, the building underwent renovations, when two more floors were added. The intervention, supervised by Luiz Moraes himself, significantly altered the original volume. Currently, Quinino houses Fiocruz central administration units.
