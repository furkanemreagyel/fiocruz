Rockefeller Pavilion

The Rockefeller Pavilion, built between 1935 and 1937, represents a pivotal shift in the architectural history of the Manguinhos campus. Designed by American professionals and funded by the Rockefeller Foundation, its functionalist and sober design broke away from the ornate, eclectic traditions previously established by Luiz Moraes Jr. As the first structure on the site to embrace the principles of modern architecture, it symbolizes the arrival of international scientific collaboration and modern laboratory standards in Brazil. Created to support yellow fever research, the pavilion remains a testament to the early 20th-century alliance between Brazilian and American public health initiatives.
