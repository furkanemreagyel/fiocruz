The Pombal

The Pombal, or Vivarium is a unique scientific structure completed around 1904 by architect Luiz Moraes Jr. to house small research animals like rabbits, guinea pigs, and birds. Its unique design includes eight circular modules made of reinforced cement, which was a new material at the time. Originally intended for carrier pigeons, these modules arrange around a central tower. Functionally, the building was designed with strict hygiene standards, separating healthy animals from those inoculated with non-hazardous materials, while external tanks supported the breeding of frogs and turtles. Today, it remains a preserved symbol of the Manguinhos Historical Site, representing the dawn of standardized laboratory animal science in Brazil.
